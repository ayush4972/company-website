A few useful answers

Before you
make a report.

Looking for help with the app?
Contact product support

What can I report?

Road damage such as potholes, broken footpaths, and flooded stretches. Add a useful photo and confirm the location. See the official app description for details.

Does submitting a report guarantee a repair?

The responsible road authority evaluates reports and carries out maintenance. The app gives you a way to report and follow progress; it does not promise a repair date.

Is every road in Nepal covered?

Active response depends on the relevant road office. The service began with Kathmandu’s federal network and is expanding in stages. Check the office shown for your road in the app.

Where do I download the app?

Use the official listings by Trasier Tech on Google Play or the App Store. These links open the store in a new tab.

How is my information handled?

Reporting uses location and account information. Read the Sadak Sujhav privacy policy for collection, use, and deletion information. You can also visit the account deletion page.
