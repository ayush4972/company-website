The Company

Built in Kathmandu,
for the public good.

Trasier Technology is a mission-driven engineering company building the digital infrastructure of everyday life in Nepal.

We don't just build apps; we architect the operational systems that modern society runs on. From citizen-facing platforms to the data infrastructure beneath them, our work sits where technology meets everyday public life, and it has to work for everyone, on any device, on any network.

Based out of Kathmandu, our focus is local but our standards are global. We believe in high-density data, low-latency governance, and the quiet power of transparent, dependable engineering. We started with a single road complaint and set out to build the infrastructure layer for an entire valley, and beyond.

Small team, high standards, real deployments. That's the whole idea.

The Founder

From the Founder

AC

Ayush Chaudhary

Founder & CEO
B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering, VIT, India
Get in touch arrow_forward

"I didn't start Trasier to build another app. I started it because a road complaint in Kathmandu had nowhere real to go, and I couldn't stop thinking about how many other broken systems were exactly the same."

That complaint became Sadak Sujhav, and Sadak Sujhav became the reason Trasier exists. It showed me something simple: the gap between citizens and the institutions meant to serve them isn't a policy problem, it's an engineering problem. Nobody had built the plumbing. So we did.

I run Trasier day to day as CEO, but I never stopped being an engineer first. I sit in on architecture decisions, pressure-test scope before it becomes a promise, and make sure whatever we ship actually solves the problem a client walked in with, not a watered-down version of it. That's the standard I hold every deployment to, whether it's a municipality's data pipeline or a citizen-facing app used by thousands of people who never think about the software, only whether it works.

Beyond delivery, I own growth: the client relationships, the partnerships with municipalities and government bodies that turn a working prototype into a real deployment, and the team we're building to carry this mission further than one person or one product ever could.

Trasier is based in Kathmandu because that's where the problem started, but the standard is global. High-density data, low-latency governance, software built to ship and stay running. Small team, high standards, real deployments. We started with roads. We're not stopping there.
