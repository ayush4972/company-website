Sadak Sujhav brings the people using a road closer to the teams looking after it. Follow the connection.

01
A person notices.

Real life is the starting point.

A damaged road. A flooded stretch. A broken footpath. A photo and a few details turn something you’ve noticed into a clear report.

Meet the citizen app
02
A place adds context.

Put the problem in the picture.

Location, a landmark, and severity give a report its context. See the road-office routing before submitting, with saved reports available when you’re offline.

Follow a report
03
A connection moves it forward.

Better information. A shared next step.

The responsible road office receives the report. People can follow its status and message within the report. The technology connects; road authorities carry out repairs.
