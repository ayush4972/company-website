Good questions

Before we build.

Do we need a large dataset?

It depends on the task. Some problems can use existing models and retrieval; others need representative training or evaluation data. We assess what you have first.

Can AI make final decisions automatically?

We design the review and decision boundaries around the risk of the task. Sensitive or consequential actions need clear human responsibility.

How do you know whether it works?

We agree examples, quality measures, and failure cases before implementation, then evaluate the system against those expectations.
