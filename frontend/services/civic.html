What we do / Civic & GovTech

Public services.
Made easier to use.

Connect the person asking for help with the team responsible for responding. We build the workflow on both sides.

The approach

A service is more than a form.

A request needs a clear path: capture the right information, route it to the right team, show what happens next, and keep a record. We design civic platforms around that complete journey.

For public bodies and service teams replacing fragmented inboxes, manual registers, and unclear handoffs with a considered digital workflow.

Discuss your project

What we can build

Scope around
what you need.

We combine the right parts into a project with clear priorities, practical constraints, and a defined outcome.

  • Citizen-facing experiences

    Accessible reporting, applications, and service requests with understandable guidance and status.

  • Case management

    Triage, assignment, staff work queues, and an agreed set of statuses for the people doing the work.

  • Accountability & access

    Role-based access, activity history, and clear ownership so a case does not disappear between teams.

  • Communication

    Notifications and updates that explain progress without asking residents to repeatedly chase an answer.

From context to delivery

A considered path forward.

01

Map the real service

Follow one request from the resident to the responsible office. Identify ownership, evidence, and the difficult handoffs.

02

Prototype both sides

Test the public journey alongside the staff workflow. Confirm that one makes the other easier.

03

Launch a usable workflow

Agree roles, reporting, training needs, and a manageable rollout before adding complexity.

Can this fit an existing administrative process?

Yes. Discovery begins with the process you have, the obligations behind it, and which parts can realistically change.

How do you approach accessibility?

We consider plain language, keyboard access, device constraints, clear forms, and the people who may need an assisted route.
