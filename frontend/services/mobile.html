Good questions

Before we build.

Do we need separate web and mobile products?

Not always. We consider your audience, distribution needs, device features, and maintenance budget before recommending a platform approach.

Can you redesign an existing application?

Yes. We start with where users get stuck, the constraints of the current architecture, and which improvements create the clearest benefit.

What happens after launch?

We agree the monitoring, documentation, handover, and ongoing support required for your product as part of the project scope.
