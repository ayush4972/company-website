Good questions

Before we build.

Can you connect a legacy system?

Often, but the available interfaces matter. We assess database access, export options, APIs, and operational limits before proposing a connector.

What if one of the services goes down?

We design appropriate timeouts, retries, queues, alerts, and recovery steps so an interruption does not silently lose work.

Will our team be able to maintain the integration?

Documentation, deployment notes, interface contracts, and an agreed handover are part of planning a maintainable integration.
