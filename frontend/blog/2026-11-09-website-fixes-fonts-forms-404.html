Website fixes: self-hosted fonts, safer forms, and a proper 404 page

A few things from last month's relaunch didn't quite hold up. Here is what we found and fixed.

By Trasier TechnologyNovember 9, 2026
On this page 5 sections
  1. Fonts, self-hosted
  2. A page for broken links
  3. A social-share image that actually loads
  4. More careful, more reliable forms
  5. Smaller logo files

Our new site went live in October. Since then we ran it through a closer audit and found a handful of things worth fixing: some cosmetic, some that affected whether a form submission or a shared link actually worked. This is what changed.

Fonts, self-hosted

The site used to pull its Manrope typeface from Google Fonts on every visit. It now ships its own licensed, locally hosted copy of the variable font, with the OFL license and font sources documented in the repository. One less outside request on every page load.

A page for broken links

A mistyped or outdated link used to land on a blank error. There is now a branded 404 page that explains what happened and points back to the site.

A social-share image that actually loads

The page previews shown when a link to the site is shared on social media or in chat were pointing at a social-share image that did not exist. There is now a real 1200×630 image behind those previews, so links to the site and to journal posts preview correctly.

More careful, more reliable forms

The contact form and careers form now reject malformed submissions before anything is sent, with a honeypot field and basic rate-limiting against automated spam. Attachments on the careers form are checked for file type and size before upload. Resubmitting the same form, for example after a slow connection, reuses the same submission instead of sending a duplicate, and a failed submission keeps what was typed instead of clearing the form.

We also raised the contrast on form borders and focus outlines and increased some of the smaller supporting text on mobile, both of which were harder to read than they should have been.

Smaller logo files

The header and footer logo now loads from properly sized 160px and 320px WebP exports instead of one larger master image. The 320px version is about 92% smaller than the original file, with no visible loss of quality.

None of this changes what the site says or what Sadak Sujhav does. It is the kind of maintenance that mostly shows up as things quietly working the way they were supposed to. If you spot something that still isn't right, tell us.
